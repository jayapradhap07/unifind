Student Security Guidelines

Campus Lost & Found Safety Protocol

Recommended procedures for safely returning or claiming missing belongings.

1. Verify Ownership First

Before handing over an item, ask the claimant for unique proof of ownership, such as:

  • Unlocking the device screen or entering password in front of you.
  • Describing specific scratches, stickers, or wallpaper.
  • Showing a matching student ID or syllabus inside a notebook.

2. Safe Handover Locations

Never meet in isolated areas or off-campus locations. Always conduct handovers at:

  • Central Library Main Circulation Desk
  • Campus Security Office (Open 24/7)
  • Student Center Information Center

3. Sensitive Items (IDs & Cards)

If you find a student ID card, driver's license, or bank card:

  • Do not publish full numbers or security codes online.
  • Turn in found bank cards directly to the Campus Security Office.
  • The security office will contact the student via official university directory.

4. Close Your Report

Once your item is recovered, click "Mark as Returned" under My Reports or in the item details view. This keeps the campus database accurate and up-to-date.
